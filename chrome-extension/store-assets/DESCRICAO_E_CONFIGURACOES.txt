TÍTULO DO PACOTE (Title):
DCPROSENS - Sensitivity Converter

RESUMO DO PACOTE (Short Description - max 132 caracteres):
Instantly convert mouse sensitivity and calculate physical cm/360 distance across 87+ shooter game engines from your side panel.

DESCRIÇÃO COMPLETA (Detailed Description — SEM LISTAS DE JOGOS OU JOGADORES):
Convert mouse sensitivity 1:1 directly from your Chrome browser side panel without leaving your active tab.

DCPROSENS preserves your physical muscle memory by calculating exact distance per 360-degree rotation across more than 87 first-person and third-person shooter game engines.

WHAT'S NEW IN VERSION 2.2.0
• Expanded engine database supporting over 87 shooter titles with instant search filtering.
• Fully unlocked 1:1 sensitivity results, one-click copy, physical cm/360°, inches/360°, and eDPI telemetry.
• Added 0% Monitor Distance Matching reference for scoped and ADS aiming.
• Added searchable professional player configuration presets and one-click crosshair code copying.
• Added console controller calibration reference and quick-launch links to browser hardware diagnostics.

FEATURES
• Precise Engine Conversions — Match sensitivity accurately between different camera rotation scales using verified engine yaw ratios.
• cm/360° & Inches/360° Calculator — Instantly measure the physical mouse travel required for a full 360-degree turn.
• eDPI & Speed Classification — Compare effective sensitivity independently of hardware DPI settings.
• Pro Feel Matcher — Calculate the matching in-game sensitivity for your mouse DPI against verified competitive presets.
• Side Panel Integration — Open anytime via toolbar icon or keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+S / Cmd+Shift+S).
• Dark & Light Themes — Clean interface with automatic local storage saving.

HOW TO USE
1. Click the DCPROSENS icon in your Chrome toolbar or press Ctrl+Shift+S (Cmd+Shift+S on Mac).
2. Choose your source and target game engines and enter your sensitivity and mouse DPI.
3. Copy your converted sensitivity and physical distance metrics in one click.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
DCPROSENS runs locally inside your browser sandbox. It does not track, collect, or transmit browsing history or personal data.
