STEP 01 // CM/360 SPECTRUM

The universal metric of physical aim.

Ditch arbitrary sliders. Measure your hand displacement in centimeters and discover which bracket fits your game.

The Universal Formula for cm/360
cm/360 = (360 × 2.54) ÷ (DPI × In-Game Sens × Yaw)

cm/360 is the exact physical distance in centimeters your hand moves horizontally on the mousepad to complete one full 360-degree rotation in-game.

STEP 02 // PSA METHOD

Finding your personal sweet spot.

A disciplined 30-minute binary search test to discover your hand's natural ergonomic sensitivity.

1

Find Your Starting Anchor (180° Comfortable Swipe)

Sit in your natural posture. Move your mouse from the center of your mousepad to the comfortable right edge. Adjust your sensitivity until this single natural stroke produces exactly a 180° rotation.

2

Bracket High vs Low (The ±50% Test)

Multiply your starting sens by 1.5 (High) and by 0.5 (Low). Play 2 minutes of tracking bots with High, then 2 minutes with Low. Record which bracket felt smoother on your wrist.

3

Perform The Strafe Tracking Test

Pick a stationary head-sized target. Keep your crosshair locked onto it while counter-strafing left and right at varying distances. If you lag behind the target, your sens is too slow; if you jitter past it, it is too fast.

4

Narrow The Range by Half (Binary Iteration)

Halve the interval each round (e.g. 1.0 ➔ 1.25 ➔ 1.125). In exactly 5 iterations, your mathematical sweet spot will emerge with zero guesswork.

Warning Against Placebo Shuffling: Never change your sensitivity multiple times in one week. Once your PSA method yields a result, lock it in for a minimum of 21 days to build deep neuromuscular myelin pathways.

STEP 03 // SENSOR TELEMETRY

Sensor DPI sweet spots & deviation.

Why pro players are switching to 1600 DPI for lower latency, and how to verify your mouse's real physical tracking accuracy.

Why 1600 DPI Beats 400 DPIINPUT LAG

At 400 DPI, your sensor must physically travel 4× farther before registering its first movement count compared to 1600 DPI. At 1600 DPI, input latency drops by ~2.5ms without increasing cursor speed if you lower in-game sens.

The Myth of Sensor SmoothingSWEET SPOT

Legacy sensors had smoothing above 1600 DPI. Modern sensors (PAW3395, Focus Pro 35K, HERO 2) remain raw and 1:1 up to 3200 DPI. 1600 DPI is the pro sweet spot.

The 10-Centimeter Physical Ruler Test (DPI Deviation)

Manufacturing tolerances in sensor lenses mean an '800 DPI' mouse might actually read at 835 DPI (+4.3%) or 765 DPI (-4.4%). Here is how to measure your true sensor DPI:

1. Tape a Ruler: Fix a physical metric ruler alongside your mousepad edge.
2. Move Exactly 10cm: Slide mouse from 0cm to 10.0cm horizontally while logging counts.
3. Calculate Deviation: Real DPI = (Counts × 2.54) ÷ 10. Adjust sens to match.
Hardware Standard: Always set Windows pointer speed to 6/11 (slider tick 6) and uncheck "Enhance Pointer Precision" to ensure pure linear 1:1 hardware translation.

STEP 04 // PIVOT MECHANICS

Arm vs wrist vs finger aiming.

How to coordinate all three anatomical levers for zero-friction movement and long-term joint health.

ARM (ELBOW PIVOT)MACRO

Used for: 180° turns, wide target transfers, and clearing large rooms.
Anchor: Forearm resting lightly on desk/pad, pivoting from the elbow joint.

WRIST (CARPAL PIVOT)TRACKING

Used for: Primary target tracking, enemy strafe compensation.
Anchor: Base of the palm resting on pad, operating within a 30° cone of motion.

FINGERS (MICRO)PRECISION

Used for: Tiny 10-pixel headshot adjustments, recoil pull-down.
Anchor: Fingertips flexing inward/outward on mouse sides for vertical nuance.

Ergonomics: The 90-Degree Forearm Rule

If your chair is too low, your shoulder shrugs upward, transferring excess downward friction to your wrist. If your desk is too low, you slouch forward, cutting off arm mobility.

A

Desk & Elbow Alignment

Set armrests/desk so your elbow forms a relaxed 90° to 100° angle with zero shoulder elevation.

B

Friction-Free Forearm

Rest 50–70% of your forearm on the desk surface. Never let your wrist dangle off the desk edge.

Core Principle: Elite aim is the synchronization of all three pivot points. The arm gets you in the neighborhood; the wrist centers the target; the fingers click the head.

STEP 07 // DAILY ACTIVATION

Pre-match calibration checklist.

A 5-minute pre-match routine to sync your hand temperature, pad glide, and hand-eye coordination before ranked play.

01 / Surface Friction & Hand Temperature Check

Ensure your hands are warm and the mousepad is free of oil/dust. Cold hands reduce finger micro-adjustment accuracy by up to 35%.

02 / The Edge-to-Edge 180° Verification

Swipe once across your pad from center to comfortable edge. Ensure your crosshair hits a perfect 180° turnaround without looking at your hand.

03 / Micro-Step Strafe Tracking (2 Minutes)

Lock your crosshair onto a bot's head while performing short A-D counter-strafes. Verify your wrist is completely relaxed.

04 / Distance Flick Reset (2 Minutes)

Perform 20 medium-range flicks between two static points. If you consistently overshoot, do NOT lower sens; let your neural paths adapt.

05 / The 21-Day Lock Discipline

Vow never to alter this value during a losing streak. A bad game is mental or tactical fatigue, not a sudden sensitivity mismatch.
