## 1. Why these 81 topics

DCPROSENS already has 119 blog articles. These 81 were selected by cross-referencing real keyword data (Semrush, US database) for direct competitors — **prosettings.net** and **mouse-sensitivity.com** — against what DCPROSENS actually has content for today, plus a direct read of `src/lib/games.ts` (72 games currently covered) and `src/lib/mouseDatabase.ts` (14 mice currently reviewed).

Every topic below is tagged with a confidence level — **be honest about this distinction when writing, it changes how the article should open**:

- **Verified** — real monthly search volume confirmed via Semrush (US). Write to rank for the exact keyword given.
- **Structural gap** — a competitor has a dedicated page for this and ranks for it, but no search-volume tool returned a number in this research session. Still worth writing (competitor coverage is itself signal), but don't fabricate a volume number in the article or its metadata.
- **Editorial extension** — no direct competitor page or confirmed keyword found; the topic follows a pattern that's already proven to work elsewhere in the data (e.g. controller-settings content clearly performs, so we extended it to more games) or fills an obvious category gap. Good content, just don't claim a specific search volume for these anywhere.

## 2. Global requirements — apply to EVERY one of the 81 articles

Do not repeat these per-article; they're listed once here and apply uniformly. The per-article table in Section 4 only lists what's *specific* to that article.

### 2.1 SEO
- **Title tag**: 50–60 characters, primary keyword as close to the front as possible, no keyword stuffing. Match (don't copy verbatim) the H1.
- **Meta description**: 140–155 characters, includes the primary keyword once, ends with a reason to click (not just a restatement of the title).
- **URL slug**: kebab-case, derived from the article title, matches the pattern already used at `dcprosens.com/blog/[slug]` (check a few existing posts in `src/lib/posts-data/*.ts` for the exact convention before generating new ones).
- **H1**: exactly one per page, matches the article title in Section 4.
- **Canonical + Open Graph + Twitter Card**: follow the existing `generateMetadata` pattern used by `src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` — do not hand-roll a different metadata shape.
- **Word count**: given per-cluster in Section 4 (ranges, not hard limits — write to the topic, not to a number).
- **Internal linking**: every article must link to at least 2 of DCPROSENS's own tools (sens converter, DPI analyzer, crosshair generator, mouse finder, Aim Lab, Aim Trainer, PSA calculator, monitor simulator — whichever are actually relevant to the topic) and at least 1 other blog post in the same cluster. Do not link to competitor sites.

### 2.2 GEO (being citable by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overview)
- **Open with a direct-answer paragraph.** Immediately after the H1, before any other content, write a 40–70 word paragraph that directly and completely answers the core question the title asks. This is the paragraph an AI assistant will lift and cite — it must stand alone without needing the rest of the article for context.
- **Use concrete numbers, not vague claims.** "Most pros use between 350–450 eDPI in CS2" is citable. "Pros tend to use lower sensitivity" is not.
- **Avoid the exact same generic framing every competitor uses.** A page that says nothing an AI Overview couldn't already synthesize from five other pages has no reason to be cited over those five other pages. Where the topic allows it (pro-player deep dives, first-mover game pages, specific product reviews), include something only DCPROSENS's own data/tools can produce — e.g. an actual conversion table generated by the site's converter, not a generic description of one.
- Every article should read as if it could be the **single source** an AI assistant cites for that exact question — not one of many interchangeable summaries.

### 2.3 AEO (featured snippets / direct-answer boxes)
- **FAQPage JSON-LD schema is required on all 81 articles** — no exceptions. (Today only ~37% of the existing 119 posts have it; this brief closes that gap for all new content.) Use the exact question text from Section 4's FAQ list as the `Question`, and a 2–4 sentence direct answer as the `Answer` — matching the schema pattern already used in the codebase (see any existing post with `faqs` populated in `src/lib/posts-data/`).
- **Use `HowTo` schema** on any article marked **[HowTo]** in Section 4 — these are genuinely step-by-step processes, not just informational reads.
- Structure the body so at least one H2's first paragraph could itself serve as a featured-snippet answer (40–50 words, directly answering that H2's implied question, before going deeper).

### 2.4 Images
Every article needs exactly one hero image. Full prompts for all 81 are in `IMAGE_PROMPTS_81_ARTICLES.md` — filenames there match the slugs generated from Section 4 titles. Do not generate images from scratch here; that file is the source of truth for visual style.

### 2.5 Brand voice
Direct, confident, data-first — this is a tool site for competitive players, not a lifestyle blog. Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). No filler intros like "In today's gaming world..." Get to the point in the first sentence. Assume the reader already knows what CS2/Valorant/DPI are — don't over-explain basics in advanced articles, but do fully explain them in the Cluster 1 (Core Definitions) articles, since those are written for beginners by design.
