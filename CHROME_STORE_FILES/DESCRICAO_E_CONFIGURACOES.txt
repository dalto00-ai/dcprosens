TÍTULO DO PACOTE (Title):
DCPROSENS - Sensitivity Converter

RESUMO DO PACOTE (Short Description - max 132 caracteres):
Instantly convert mouse sensitivity and calculate physical cm/360 distance across FPS game engines from your side panel.

DESCRIÇÃO COMPLETA (Detailed Description - SEM PALAVRAS-CHAVE EXCESSIVAS):
Convert mouse sensitivity on-the-fly directly from your Chrome browser side panel without leaving your active tab.

DCPROSENS preserves your physical muscle memory 1-to-1 by calculating exact distance per 360-degree rotation across popular FPS game engines.

FEATURES
• Precise Engine Conversions — Accurately match sensitivity between different game rotation scales using exact 3D engine yaw ratios.
• cm/360 & Inches/360 Calculator — Instantly calculate the physical distance your mouse must travel on your pad to complete a full 360° turn.
• eDPI Calculator — Compare effective sensitivity regardless of your mouse hardware DPI settings.
• Pro Player Feel Matcher — Match your eDPI against professional esports player configurations.
• Side Panel Integration — Open instantly via keyboard shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+S / Cmd+Shift+S) or toolbar icon.
• Dark & Light Modes — Dynamic theme toggle with automatic local storage saving.

HOW TO USE
1. Click the DCPROSENS icon in your browser toolbar or press Ctrl+Shift+S (Cmd+Shift+S on Mac) to open the side panel.
2. Select your source game engine and target game engine.
3. Enter your current sensitivity and mouse DPI.
4. View your converted sensitivity and physical distance calculations instantly.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
DCPROSENS runs 100% locally inside your browser sandbox. It does not track, collect, or transmit any personal browsing data or usage history.
